The shop

Everything built under one roof

A plant of about 25,000 square feet on Black Forest Road in Hull, set up to cut, form, weld and paint in steel and aluminum.

Cut

Two CNC plasma tables with water tables, a 12′ shear and tube cutters.

Form

12′ 200-ton and 14′ 300-ton press brakes, plus a tube bender.

Weld

Steel and aluminum welding stations for trailers, wagons and gates.

Paint

Two large paint rooms for primer and polyurethane finishes.
