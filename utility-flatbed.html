Cargo & skid loader trailers

A cargo hauler like no other

The K&O cargo trailer begins with the same rugged framework as our heaviest-built livestock trailer, in steel or aluminum. With 1/8″-thick aluminum extrusion side panels, a hammer bounces off.

Load your skid loader and your job-site tools on one trailer: haul the loader behind the cargo area, or choose a cargo tough enough to carry it inside. A super-heavy-duty ramp, available in bumper or gooseneck style and in various sizes.
