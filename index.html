Why Pace Setter

Heavy where it counts

2″×9″ main frame

Aluminum Pace Setters ride on a 2″×9″ all-aluminum extrusion with a ¼″ support lip under each I-beam.

.065 roof, welded

A full .065 roof, wrapped and welded to the roof bows — not taped, caulked or pop-riveted.

Rumber floors

One of the first trailer builders to use Rumber recycled-rubber floor — no mats to haul out.

Built to order

Stall width, tie location, flooring, color: fax or email a rough drawing and get a to-the-penny quote.

Harvest

Move more grain with fewer trips

The Pace Setter Grain Express is a 400-plus-bushel gooseneck grain mover with axles from 10,000 to 16,000 lb, electric brakes on both axles and a gravity unload that empties in under 89 seconds.
